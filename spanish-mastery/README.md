# How to use Spanish Mastery

Spanish Mastery runs through conversation in Codex. Your learning records live in this folder. You do not need to run commands, edit data files, or open a separate application to practice.

## Where questions and answers go

The tutor asks questions in the Codex chat. Type your answers in the same chat message box you used to request this guide, then send them. You can ask questions, explain your thinking, or say that you are unsure there too.

Usually you will receive one manageable exercise at a time. Wait for the next prompt after answering. The files are your persistent notebook; you do not need to type answers into them.

## Starting or resuming a session

Open this project in Codex and send a message such as:

> Resume Spanish Mastery. Read spanish-mastery/AGENTS.md and my learning records. I have 15 minutes today.

Use this message in a new chat too. Keeping the project folder available lets the tutor recover your learning context from the files even when the earlier conversation is unavailable.

The tutor will read your profile and progress, check any reviews due, and choose an activity. You can also request conversation, creative writing, a progress review, or help with something you encountered outside Codex.

## Your first diagnostic

Your first writing prompt is already in this conversation and is saved in sessions.md. Reply here with how much time you have and your short Spanish adventure. You can also say “Please repeat the first diagnostic prompt.”

We will collect a small baseline before targeted instruction or corrections. Write using what you currently know. If you use a dictionary, translator, hints, or other help, mention it so the record accurately reflects the conditions.

Later diagnostic prompts will ask you to express particular meanings without naming the grammar being investigated. This helps explore both errors and possible avoidance. A valid alternative expression will not automatically count as avoidance.

## How records get updated

As the tutor processes your replies, it saves completed responses and relevant observations to the project files. At the end of a session, it updates the progress summary and session history. You do not need to request these updates after every answer.

Updates are made by the tutor during active Codex conversations. There is no background service watching for practice or sending reminders. Review dates are checked when you return. Practice outside Codex becomes part of the record when you report it here.

| File | What it contains |
|---|---|
| [learner-profile.md](learner-profile.md) | Your goals, preferences, and relevant background. |
| [progress.md](progress.md) | Current priorities, supported progress estimates, reviews due, and next steps. Start here to inspect your progress. |
| [sessions.md](sessions.md) | Session summaries, pending activities, and outside practice instructions. |
| [evidence.jsonl](evidence.jsonl) | Original responses and structured observations, hypotheses, assessments, and intervention decisions. Initially empty until there is evidence to record. |
| [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) | Instructions the tutor follows for teaching, assessment, and record keeping. |

This README explains how to participate; the five files above remain the working learning system.

Original evidence is preserved. If you disagree with an evaluation or notice an inaccurate record, say so in chat. The tutor will investigate, append a correction when appropriate, and revise the current summary without silently deleting the earlier evidence.

## What progress means

Accuracy and retention are tracked separately from evidence of spontaneous use. Correct written answers do not by themselves prove that a pattern is automatic in speech.

For a confirmed difficulty, the tutor records the suspected cause, selected practice method, independent test results, and a decision to continue or change the method. Delayed checks and unfamiliar contexts help determine whether learning lasts and transfers. One successful quiz does not establish mastery.

## Ending a session or reporting outside practice

To finish, send:

> That's all for today. Please close this session.

The tutor will summarize and save the completed work, unresolved questions, and recommended next activity. Completed exchanges should also be saved along the way; an unfinished exercise is not a failure. If the app closes before a reply has been processed and saved, that latest exchange may need to be supplied again.

For outside practice, describe what you did, any exact Spanish you remember, feedback you received, and whether you consciously thought through rules. For example:

> I spoke with my exchange partner for ten minutes. I said ___. They suggested ___. I had to think through the wording / it came out without much thought.

Outside reports and partner feedback are labeled by source. They can inform practice without being presented as directly observed speaking performance.

## Useful messages

- “I have five minutes. What should we practice?”
- “I don't understand the question. Can you clarify it?”
- “I used a dictionary for these two words.”
- “I chose this wording because I wasn't sure how to express the other version.”
- “Show me my current progress and the evidence behind it.”
- “I think that correction changes what I meant.”
- “Give me a short activity for my language exchange partner.”
